## Why these choices

- **Fix rules before data.** Hypotheses, the universe rule and the D2 uptime threshold are all written down before results exist, so the results can't bend them. This is the same idea as pre-registration in science.
- **Ask "how do they differ", not "who wins".** 60 trading days is too short to prove one strategy beats another, but plenty to measure behaviour precisely.
- **Same information for everyone.** If AI agents got news and the rules didn't, any difference could come from the news, not from the AI.
