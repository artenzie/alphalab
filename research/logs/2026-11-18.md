# Wed 18 Nov 2026: daily runner, first real run

## What was built
- `runner/daily.py`: one command for a full equity or crypto day in protocol order (ingest, settle, snapshot, decide, risk, orders). It is restart-safe, writes a heartbeat for each step, and supports late runs (v1.13).
- `runner/experiment.py`: one labelled DB file per experiment.
- `analysis/results.py`: the results report. It counts late and missed days, and `--exclude-late` drops them.
- Protocol v1.13:
  - the late-run rule;
  - one DB per experiment;
  - the crypto fill hour starts at t_ref, the time the day's last crypto decision was recorded, with one t_ref per day shared by C0 and C1;
  - a coin with no fill bar is cancelled;
  - a missed day (the next cutoff passed, or the day's orders were already placed);
  - a known limitation in §9 (a fill can land after the 00:00Z mark).
- On-time crypto settles are byte-identical to v1.12: the golden fixture `bd879e9` is in a regression test.
