# Thu 26 Nov 2026

## Outage catch-up: missed, not error (protocol v1.17)
- **What happened.** The Pi was off from Sat 21 Nov 22:41Z to Thu 26 Nov 20:05Z (clean shutdown). On boot the tick caught up.
  - 25 Nov ran late and ok in both asset classes (correct).
  - 23 and 24 Nov (equity and crypto) were held, settled and marked correctly, but `runner.status` showed them as `error`.
- **Root causes.**
  1. Past the deadline, `build_snapshot` and `decide` wrote `event='error'`. Every error event made the run status `error`, so a correct hold looked like a failure. This alone explains crypto 23/24.
  2. Equity only: `network-online.target` was reached at 20:05:27, but wlan0 only connected at 20:10:04. The 23/24 equity ingests failed with `ConnectionError` (3 attempts each); 25 Nov's third attempt got through.
  3. A day past its deadline got one settle-only try, then was left alone as a "gap". The marks survived only because every agent was in cash; with positions, the failed fetch would have left 23 Nov unmarked for good, and `settle_day(24)` doesn't refuse in that case.
  4. Past the deadline the margin check was skipped (`placed = "past deadline"`), contrary to §3.
  5. Alerts: `missed:` keys were re-opened by every tick after their RECOVERED resolve. That was 8 phone alerts per tick: 33 queued and 33 sent between 20:11 and 20:20Z. Stopgap: `runner.tick deactivate` at 20:25Z (not commenting out NTFY_TOPIC, which would only have queued them).
- **Fix (approved by Artem).**
  - Protocol v1.17 states the principle: the ledger's mechanical steps (settle, accruals, mark, margin check) apply on the protocol schedule regardless of when they are computed, with the real computation time recorded; no agent ever decides after its deadline.
  - Past-deadline runs end `missed` and get the margin check. Forced reductions are stamped at the day's protocol decision time, with `computed_at` in the risk event and the heartbeat.
  - A day the ledger already moved past (left by the old code) is checked without writing; a breach there is an error for a manual correction entry.
  - Settle-only runs retry every tick until the day is missed; later days of the asset class wait for missing marks.
  - Network check: one HTTPS request to data.alpaca.markets, 5 s, no credentials. Offline, the tick runs nothing and uses no attempt.
  - `runner.status`: `missed` outcome, counted against uptime.
  - Alerts: `missed:` opens once ever. 2 failed settle-only runs past the deadline open `error:<ac>:<date>` once, high priority.
- **Tests.** `tests/test_outage.py` covers:
