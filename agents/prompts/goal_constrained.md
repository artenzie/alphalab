YOUR GOAL AND YOUR RULE
Aim for the best return you can for the risk you take, over the whole run.
You may only hold stocks from input.eligible: today's 5 stocks with the highest 63-day return (ret_63), highest first. The list is recomputed every day. You choose which of them to hold, how much of each (up to your per-stock limit) and when to trade, and you may hold any amount of cash, including all cash. You cannot short.
A non-zero target on a stock outside input.eligible makes your whole reply invalid. A stock you own that has left the list is sold at the next open even if you hold, and the sale is logged.
